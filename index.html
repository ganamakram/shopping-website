How do I place an order on the website?

Simply select your favorite item, choose the quantity, and click on the "confirm purchase" button to complete your order.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Credit/Debit cards, and online wallet payments.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery usually takes between 2 to 5 business days depending on your location

Can I track my order status?

Yes, once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email to monitor your package.

What is your return and exchange policy?

You can return or exchange any item within 14 days of receiving it, provided it is unused and in its original condition.

Are the products true to size?

Yes, all our clothes fit standard sizing. You can also check our size guide on each product page for precise measurements.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only offer local shipping within the country. International shipping will be available soon!

How can I cancel or modify my order?

You can cancel or modify your order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting our support team via the Contact page.

What should I do if I receive a damaged item?

If you receive a defective or damaged product, please contact us immediately with a photo of the item, and we will send a replacement free of charge.

How can I contact customer service?

You can reach us through our Contact Us page, or by sending an email directly to our support team.
